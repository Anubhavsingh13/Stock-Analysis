## Business Primer
Ashapura mines and processes industrial minerals through two very different engines. **India (value-added):** bentonite (oil & gas drilling fluids, foundry, agriculture), bleaching clay (edible-oil refining), kaolin (paint/fiberglass), specialty absorbents and refractories — margin-oriented, ONGC relationship since 1987. **Guinea, West Africa (bulk volume):** large-scale bauxite mining and export, now the primary revenue driver after a decade of phased investment in roads, mine access, transhipment and captive port infrastructure (record ~2 MT quarterly bauxite exports in FY26). Revenue is fundamentally **tonnes × realization**, exposed simultaneously to global bauxite/alumina prices, ocean-freight rates and USD/INR. The **consolidated** entity (FY26 revenue ₹5,237 Cr, NP ₹416 Cr) — not the small standalone (₹432 Cr) — is the real business; the gap is the overseas mining/logistics subsidiaries.

This is a **cyclical commodity / mining** business, value-driven by **volume (Guinea ramp) + commodity price + freight**. The key risks stack up: commodity-cycle exposure at what looks like a **peak**, **Guinea geographic/geopolitical concentration**, working-capital-intensive bulk trade, and — decisively — a **governance-impaired promoter group**: near-death BIFR/derivatives crisis (FY11 negative net worth, $36.3m shipping-arbitration loss), a 2014–16 family control dispute, an IBC filing an appellate tribunal reversed as improper, and MD Chetan Shah's personal criminal conviction (Oct 2024, under appeal) in the Karnataka Belekeri iron-ore case. This history colours every line of the analysis.

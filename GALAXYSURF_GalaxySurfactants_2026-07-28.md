## Executive Summary
Galaxy Surfactants is **India's largest oleochemical-based surfactant maker** and a genuine, institutionally-owned quality franchise — a B2B ingredients supplier of performance and specialty surfactants to the world's home- and personal-care majors (HUL, P&G, Unilever, L'Oréal, Colgate, Henkel etc.), with plants in India, Egypt and the US and 215+ products. Through the cycle it compounds at ~**18% ROE (10-yr)** on strong, consistent cash generation and a fortress balance sheet. It is currently in a **cyclical soft patch** — FY26 revenue rose 24% to ₹5,245 Cr but that is largely raw-material (fatty alcohol/palm) price inflation; **volume growth was mid-single-digit, EBITDA/MT slipped, margin fell to 9%, and PAT dipped to ₹267 Cr** (from the ₹381 Cr FY23 peak) on RM inflation, soft global demand and tariff noise. The right lens is **EBITDA per metric ton and specialty-mix growth**, not revenue. At ~25x P/E / 2.5x book (down ~29% from its ₹2,750 high), it is fairly-to-fully valued for its quality — a business worth owning, best accumulated on further weakness or a per-ton-margin recovery.

## Module 1 — Broad Market, Sector & Stock Cycle
- **Broad market:** Nifty near highs; Optimistic. Normal sizing.
- **Sector cycle (surfactants / HPC ingredients):** Demand is **defensive-staple** (home & personal care) with steady mid-single-digit volume growth from penetration + premiumisation. The margin cycle is driven by **fatty alcohol / palm-derivative** raw-material swings, which Galaxy largely passes through — so per-ton margin (EBITDA/MT) is the true health metric, not revenue. Currently a **mild HEADWIND**: RM inflation, soft global (esp. developed-market) demand, and US tariff uncertainty are compressing per-ton economics. **Structural tailwind intact:** premiumisation, clean-label/mild surfactants, preservatives, and Americas specialty traction.
- **Stock cycle:** **Mid-cycle quality compounder in a soft patch.** Revenue optically strong (RM-inflated), volumes resilient (India +8%), specialty volumes +27% — the durable value driver. FCF positive; no capex or balance-sheet stress. Classification favours accumulation on weakness rather than chasing.

## Module 3 — Business Quality & Moat
**Greenwald barriers:** (1) Cost/scale — **Yes**, India's largest, backward integration, global footprint. (2) Customer captivity — **Yes**, multi-year qualification into global FMCG formulations = high switching costs; the single strongest moat element. (3) Specialty IP — preservatives, mild/syndet surfactants, silicone specialties for premium personal care.
**Moat verdict: NARROW-to-MODERATE (genuine).** Buffett test: pricing power is per-ton-disciplined rather than absolute (pass-through model), but **stable EBITDA/MT across cycles proves durable value capture.** Specialty mix (~35% and rising) steadily deepens the moat.

## Module 6 — Earnings Quality, ROIC & DuPont
- **Returns:** ROE 10-yr **18%**, 5-yr 15%, 3-yr 13%, FY26 ~11% (cycle low). ROCE FY26 13.5% (above WACC). A **genuine value creator** even at the trough.
- **DuPont (FY26):** ROE ≈ Net margin 5.1% × Asset turnover 1.36x × Equity multiplier 1.41x. The thin *net margin on revenue* is a feature of the pass-through model, not weakness — which is exactly why **EBITDA/MT (~₹20,000/MT) is the correct KPI**. Per-ton margin softened modestly in FY26 (RM inflation, mix/geography), the crux of the current soft patch.
- **Earnings quality: HIGH** — cash-backed, transparent, cyclically soft but structurally sound.

## Module 9 — Valuation
- **P/E ~25x on softened FY26 EPS ₹75.4; P/B 2.53x; div yield 1.12%.** EV/EBITDA modest given low debt + treasury.
- On **normalised (mid-cycle) earnings** — PAT back toward ₹330–380 Cr as per-ton margin recovers — forward P/E compresses to ~18–20x, reasonable for an 18%-ROE compounder.
- **PIE:** market is pricing a **cyclical earnings recovery** off the FY26 dip — a reasonable base case for this franchise, so the gap is roughly *fair* rather than a bargain.
- **MoS:** Modest at ₹1,960. For a quality name a 20–25% discount to intrinsic is enough; the stock is **~29% off its ₹2,750 high**, giving *some* cushion but not a deep-value entry. **Preferred accumulation zone ~₹1,600–1,800**, or on confirmed EBITDA/MT recovery.

## Module 11 — Forward View & Decision
**Thesis:** own a durable, cash-generative HPC-ingredients leader through a cyclical margin trough, with specialty mix and Americas as the structural growth engines.
**Catalysts:** EBITDA/MT recovery as RM stabilises; specialty-mix + Americas scaling; volume acceleration; treasury/dividend optionality.
**Risks:** prolonged RM inflation / palm volatility; developed-market demand & tariffs; per-ton margin staying soft; currency.

**Decision: WATCHLIST / ACCUMULATE ON WEAKNESS.** Conviction **6/10**. A clear quality franchise (clean books, 18% through-cycle ROE, fortress balance sheet, sticky global customers) at a fair-to-full price in a cyclical soft patch. Not a table-pounding bargain at ₹1,960, but a high-quality name to accumulate toward **₹1,600–1,800** or on a confirmed per-ton-margin inflection. Starter position acceptable now for quality-focused investors; add on weakness.
**Suggested size:** 2–3% starter; scale to 4–5% on better entry / margin recovery.

## Executive Summary
Ratnaveer is a thin-margin stainless-steel *processor* (sheets, washers, pipes, wire) growing revenue fast (24% 5-yr CAGR) but doing so on the back of relentless capital consumption — free cash flow has been negative in 6 of the last 7 years, it raised ~₹300 cr of fresh equity in FY26, added ₹140 cr of debt, and is *still* raising ₹330 cr more via a rights issue. Returns (ROIC ≈ WACC ≈ 12%) mean the growth creates almost no per-share value, and the new Copper-Clad-Laminate (CCL) diversification pushes it into an unrelated, subsidy-dependent electronics business it has never run. With the stock at a fresh 52-week high (₹206, up 13% intraday) there is **no margin of safety**, and the expected 2-year return is roughly flat once rights dilution is factored in.

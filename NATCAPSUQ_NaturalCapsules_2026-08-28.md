## Executive Summary
Natural Capsules is India's #2 gelatin capsule-shell maker — a genuinely profitable, export-oriented core business — that has taken on heavy debt to build a greenfield steroidal-**API** plant (Natural Biogenex, Tumkur) which is currently deeply loss-making and has pushed the *consolidated* entity into losses (FY26 net loss ₹25 cr), with **near-zero cash and an active fundraising need**. The entire investment thesis rests on the API ramp turning profitable — into a market where Chinese oversupply is crushing realisations — while the company is simultaneously under liquidity and dilution pressure. The core is real; the transformation is unproven and financed on a stretched balance sheet.

## BUSINESS PRIMER

**What it does / how it makes money.** Natural Capsules (Bangalore, incorporated 1993) manufactures **empty capsule shells** — both hard **gelatin** capsules and **HPMC** (vegetarian) capsules — which it sells B2B to pharmaceutical and nutraceutical companies in India and ~28 export countries (exports ≈ 29% of revenue). Revenue is essentially volume × realisation of billions of capsule shells; installed capacity is now ~20.25 billion capsules/annum. This capsule business is the historical, profitable heart of the company. Since ~FY22 the company has been building a **second, unrelated business — active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), specifically steroids** — through subsidiary Natural Biogenex at a new greenfield Tumkur facility (built to WHO-GMP / USFDA / EU-GMP, zero-liquid-discharge), funded largely by term debt. The API business is pre-scale, loss-making, and is the swing factor for the whole equity.

**What kind of business / what to watch.** This is a **small-cap (₹223 cr mcap) asset-heavy manufacturer mid-transformation** — a stable, cash-generative commodity-ish core (capsules) bolted to a capital-hungry, cyclically ill-timed new venture (steroid APIs). The core capsule business is a low-to-mid-teens-margin, working-capital-heavy commodity with modest pricing power and a currency tailwind; the API business is a classic "capex-into-oversupply" bet dependent on backward integration, regulatory approvals, and PLI incentives. The single most important structural feature: **standalone (capsules) is profitable but consolidated (capsules + API + interest) is loss-making** — so the reported headline loss overstates the health of the core, while the balance-sheet risk (₹162 cr debt, ~zero cash, fundraise pending) understates how much must go right for equity holders to win.

## Module 10 — Forward View: 2–3 Year Thesis

**What must go right (all of them):**
1. API (Tumkur) reaches breakeven and then profitability despite Chinese oversupply — via backward integration, enzymatic-route cost cuts, WHO-GMP approval (expected end-Q2 FY27), and CMO contracts (Fermbox, Mumbai client).
2. **PLI incentives** (~₹67 cr over 6 years, ~₹11 cr/yr, 3 steroidal APIs) actually flow and materially cushion the scale-up.
3. The fundraise is **modest and non-dilutive-ish** (or debt-based) — not a deep discounted equity raise.
4. Core capsule margins hold at ~13% and exports keep benefiting from a soft rupee.
5. Interest cost (~₹20 cr/yr) gets covered as API contributes.

**Catalysts:** WHO-GMP audit clearance (Q2 FY27); API volume ramp + first profitable API quarter; PLI disbursement visibility; a clean/non-dilutive funding solution.

**Risks (high):** Deeper API losses / delayed breakeven; a **large discounted equity raise** (dilution); interest-coverage breach; China price war intensifying; HPMC demand staying weak; contingent-liability crystallisation; promoter holding drifting lower.

**Bull / Base / Bear (2–3 yr):**
- **Bull (~20%):** API breaks even by FY27-end, scales to profit + PLI flows, capsule steady → consolidated PAT recovers to ₹20–30 cr, re-rates → ₹350–450.
- **Base (~45%):** API losses narrow slowly, one more (dilutive) raise, capsule carries the company; consolidated near-breakeven → stock range-bound ₹180–260.
- **Bear (~35%):** API breakeven slips, deep dilutive raise and/or covenant stress, China pricing worsens → toward capsule-only value ₹110–140.

Expected value skews toward the base/bear given the asymmetry and financing risk → **no adequate margin of safety at ₹214.**

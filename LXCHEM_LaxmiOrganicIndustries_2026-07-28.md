## Executive Summary
Laxmi Organic is a genuine, established mid-cap specialty-chemicals franchise — **India's largest acetyl-intermediates (ethyl acetate) maker, 3rd globally ex-China, and the only domestic diketene-derivatives producer (~55% share)** — now diversifying into **fluorospecialty chemicals** (via the Miteni technology acquisition) as its future growth engine. Unlike many small-caps, its accounting is clean and it generates real operating cash flow. The problem is that it is deep in an **earnings downcycle**: PAT has fallen every year from the ₹256 Cr FY22 peak to **₹79 Cr in FY26 (−30% YoY)**, operating margin has compressed from 12% to **6%**, and **ROCE has collapsed from 26% (FY21) to ~5%** — all while the company pours debt-funded capital into capex (borrowings ₹143→543 Cr in two years; CWIP ₹652 Cr) that has yet to earn a return. A CFO resignation (Apr-2026) and drifting promoter stake add governance noise. At **~67x trailing earnings (~30x on normalised)** the market is already paying for a recovery + fluoro optionality that has not shown up in the numbers. Real franchise, wrong point in the cycle and price — wait for an earnings inflection.

- **Secular vs. cyclical:** The margin compression is largely **cyclical/structural-oversupply** (should mean-revert *if and when* capacity rationalises), overlaid on a durable long-term demand base (solvents, pharma, agrochem, coatings). - **Sector verdict: HEADWIND now, with a capital-cycle turn as the eventual (untimed) catalyst.** Note: capacity is *still being added*, not yet exiting — the classic pre-condition for a cycle turn (capacity destruction) is not yet visible.

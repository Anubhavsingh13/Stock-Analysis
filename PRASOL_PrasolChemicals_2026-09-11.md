## Executive Summary
Prasol is a 30-year-old specialty & performance-chemicals maker with genuine niche depth — **150+ products across phosphorus chemistry (53 products), acetone derivatives (21), and customised surfactants/esters/ethers**, sold to 1,107 customers across 69 countries with a 93% repeat-customer base and a standout position as **India's largest acetone importer**. The trajectory is the attraction: revenue ₹877 → ₹1,012 → ₹1,233 cr and PAT ₹18 → ₹44 → ₹83 cr over FY24–26, with EBITDA margin climbing from 6.9% to 11.3% and ROE reaching 20.4%. But two things temper it: **(1) operating cash flow has been falling even as profit rises (₹115.6 cr FY24 → ₹49.5 cr FY26)** — an earnings-quality divergence to watch closely; and **(2) at ~43–47x earnings on an 11% EBITDA margin, the IPO is priced for continued flawless margin expansion.** A decent, improving business — but the price leaves no margin of safety, and the cash-flow signal warrants confirmation as a listed company. **Watchlist, don't chase.**

## Business Primer
Prasol makes money by converting bought-in feedstock (notably acetone, which it imports at scale, plus phosphorus) into a broad basket of specialty and performance chemicals used in **pharmaceuticals, agrochemicals, paints & coatings, personal care and performance additives.** Revenue is **volume × realization across 150+ SKUs**, split between phosphorus-based products, acetone-based products, and customised molecules; it is a mix of catalogue specialty chemicals and made-to-spec supply, ~exported to 69 countries. The economic engine is **chemistry know-how + backward feedstock access (acetone import scale) + a sticky, repeat customer base (93%).** What it depends on: feedstock cost/availability (acetone spreads), the ability to keep shifting mix toward higher-value molecules, and regulatory/quality compliance for pharma/agrochem customers.

This is a **mid-tier specialty chemical** — better than a commodity, not yet in the top bracket (SRF/PI/Navin) whose 20–30% EBITDA margins signal true value-added chemistry. At 11.3% EBITDA, Prasol still sits closer to the **semi-specialty** band; the investment question is whether the margin climb (6.9%→11.3%) is a durable up-mix or a cyclical/feedstock-timing benefit. Key value driver: margin/mix. Key risks: **feedstock (acetone) price swings, customer/end-market cyclicality (agrochem destocking), rising contingent liabilities (₹109 cr), high employee attrition (28%), and the falling operating cash flow.** Colouring feature: a strong growth optics record but a **widening gap between reported profit and cash generation** that must be reconciled before trusting the earnings.

## Module 4/5 — Forensics & Earnings Quality (the crux)
- **OCF/PAT divergence: OCF ₹115.6 cr (FY24) → ₹49.5 cr (FY26) while PAT went ₹18 → ₹83 cr.** Profit up 4.6x, operating cash down ~57%. **This is the single most important flag** — it says the reported profit growth is not converting to cash; likely working-capital build (receivables/inventory) funding the growth. O'Glove flag #1 triggered squarely.
- **Rising contingent liabilities ₹109 cr** — investigate nature (tax/litigation/guarantees) in DRHP.
- Debt/equity 0.23x (₹344 cr borrowings) — balance sheet not stretched; debtor days ~70 (reasonable for specialty chem).
- **High attrition 28%** — operational/culture flag for a chemistry business dependent on process know-how.
- Net: **1 severe flag (cash conversion) + 2 soft flags.** Requires 2–3 listed quarters to confirm the cash gap is a growth artifact that reverses, not a structural quality problem.

## Module 8 — Valuation
- **PIE:** ~43–47x P/E implies the market expects margins to keep expanding toward the high-teens AND revenue to compound ~20%+ — i.e., a full re-rate into the specialty-leader bracket. That is an **optimistic gap**; matching it merely earns cost of capital.
- **Own base case:** FY28 revenue ~₹1,650–1,800 cr (15–18% CAGR), EBITDA margin holding ~11–12% (conservative), PAT ~₹115–135 cr. On a fair 25–30x (justified for genuine but not top-tier specialty) → fair value materially below ₹676. **MoS negative at IPO price.**
- Bull case (margins to 14–15%, mix-up succeeds, cash converts) supports a higher multiple — but that is the thing to *observe*, not assume.

## Verdict
**WATCHLIST — Quality Improving, Price Full.** Prasol is a legitimately improving specialty-chemical franchise with a diversified customer base and a real phosphorus/acetone niche, but it is **priced at a top-tier multiple (~43–47x) on semi-specialty economics (11% EBITDA), with a worrying decline in operating cash flow.**
- **Move to BUY** only if, as a listed company, it delivers: (a) **2–3 quarters of OCF ≥ PAT** (cash gap was a growth artifact), (b) EBITDA margin holding/expanding toward mid-teens, and (c) a de-rate or earnings catch-up bringing the multiple toward ~25–30x.
- **Do not chase at ₹676.** Conviction 5/10.
- **Review trigger:** operating cash flow vs PAT each quarter; acetone spread; contingent-liability resolution; attrition trend.

## Executive Summary
Valiant Organics is a **diversified specialty-chemical intermediates manufacturer** (chlorophenols, anilines, anisidines, para-amino-phenol) serving dyes/pigments, agrochemicals and pharma. It was a high-return franchise (26–27% EBITDA margins, ROCE ~18% in FY19–23) that was **hammered in FY24–25 by Chinese dumping and a collapse in dye/pigment/agro-intermediate prices** — two straight loss years. The thesis is a **turnaround that is already underway and visible in the numbers**: FY26 swung back to a ₹33 Cr profit, EBITDA margin recovered +447 bps to ~12%, and Q4 FY26 was the strongest quarter of the cycle (₹16 Cr PAT, 12.2% margin). Crucially — and unlike Thirumalai Chemicals — **the balance sheet is safe (net D/E ~0.33×) and the heavy capex is already behind it** (CWIP has fallen to a trivial ₹21 Cr), so this is a *harvest-and-deleverage* story, not a leveraged bet. The catch: at ₹291 the stock has **already rallied ~50% off its ₹195 low**, trades at ~1.07× book and ~28× trailing (13–15× on recovered/normalised earnings), and **ROCE at ~5.5% is still below cost of capital** — the recovery is real but not yet complete, and the sharp jump in receivables (debtor days 90→120) is a quality flag that must clear before this earns a full position.

## BUSINESS PRIMER

Valiant Organics makes **specialty chemical intermediates** through a suite of chemistries — chlorination, hydrogenation, ammonolysis, acetylation, sulphonation and methoxylation — at six plants across five Gujarat/Maharashtra locations (70,000 TPA total). Its products are building-block molecules: **chlorophenols** (PCP, OCP, DCP, TCP — for agrochemicals, veterinary, cosmetics), **anilines and nitroanilines** (PNA, OCPNA — for dyes/pigments), **anisidines** (ortho/para — dyes), and **para-amino-phenol (PAP)** plus pharma intermediates (paracetamol chain). Revenue is **volume × realisation** of these intermediates, sold B2B to industrial customers. By end-market, FY26 revenue splits ~**Dyes & Pigments 45%, Agrochemicals 24%, Pharmaceuticals 21%, Specialty 10%**; by chemistry, Hydrogenation 48%, Ammonolysis 26%, Chlorination 20%. The company's stated niche is molecules with "high demand but low supply, primarily dependent on imports" — i.e., **import-substitution intermediates** where it is often one of the few or largest domestic players.

This is a **cyclical specialty-chemical intermediates business — better than a pure commodity but not a branded compounder.** It has some differentiation (multi-chemistry integration, ZLD plants, few domestic competitors in several products, largest global chlorophenol-derivatives maker) but limited pricing power against **Chinese competition**, which is the swing factor for both volume and price. The key value drivers are **capacity utilisation and the spread between realisations and raw-material (benzene/phenol-chain) costs**; the key risk is **Chinese dumping / import parity** in its dye-pigment and agro-intermediate lines, which is exactly what caused the FY24–25 downturn. Two structural features colour the analysis: (1) a **listed associate, Valiant Laboratories (VLL)** — a former step-down subsidiary that IPO'd and diluted to 46.83%, now equity-accounted (small drag); and (2) an **emerging Pharma segment (PAP + intermediates)**, PLI-eligible, that is the main forward-integration growth option. Standalone and consolidated are close (subsidiaries are small), so the consolidated statements are clean and representative.

## MODULE 1b — Sector Cycle: Tailwinds & Headwinds

**Secular vs. cyclical.** The FY24–25 collapse was **cyclical** (Chinese oversupply + destocking in dyes/pigments and agrochemicals crushing prices), and the FY26 recovery is **early-cyclical mean-reversion** helped by company self-help (cost optimisation, RM efficiency). The durable secular baseline is modest single-digit volume growth + **import-substitution share gains** (China+1, PLI for PAP). **Do not extrapolate the FY19–21 26–27% margins** — those were a one-off windfall from China's environmental-crackdown supply shock and will not recur; normalised through-cycle EBITDA margin is more like **13–16%**.

**Input cost cycle:** Benzene/phenol-chain feedstocks stabilising; RM intensity fell in FY26 (a tailwind management explicitly cited).

**Competitive capacity / imports — the core risk:** Chinese capacity overhang in dye/pigment and agro intermediates is structural. Recovery depends on Chinese pricing discipline and any anti-dumping protection; a fresh China price war is the main downside.

**Sector cycle verdict: NEUTRAL, turning UP from a trough.** Early recovery confirmed but not yet robust. → modest MoS widening for residual China risk.

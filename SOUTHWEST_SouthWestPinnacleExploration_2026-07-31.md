## Business Primer
Incorporated in 2006 and Gurugram-based, South West Pinnacle Exploration sells exploratory and production **drilling services**: it deploys rigs and geologists to explore for coal, base and precious minerals and coal-bed methane, drills water/geothermal wells, and executes geotechnical and (increasingly) mine developer-cum-operator (MDO) contracts. Customers are dominated by government/PSU bodies — Coal India subsidiaries (CMPDI), state mineral corporations, MECL, Oil India (seismic empanelment) — plus a growing private-sector book (Hindustan Zinc subsidiary, gold explorers in Andhra Pradesh). Revenue is project/contract-based: order book × rig deployment × execution velocity, billed on metres drilled or contract milestones.

This is an **asset-heavy, working-capital-intensive services** business tied to a project pipeline. The value driver is order inflow converted to executed metres at healthy margins; the key risks are receivable cycles with government clients (already 175 days), rig capex and utilisation, monsoon seasonality (Q1/Jun quarter is structurally weak), and concentration in PSU tenders. The structural feature colouring the whole analysis is a **strong, policy-driven demand backdrop** — the National Critical Minerals Mission, coal-block auctions, and the drive for mineral self-reliance are multi-year tailwinds that expand the addressable exploration market and are pulling the company up-market into larger MDO contracts.

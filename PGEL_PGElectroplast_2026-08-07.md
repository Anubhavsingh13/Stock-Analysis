## Business Primer

PG Electroplast is India's largest room-air-conditioner **ODM/OEM contract manufacturer** and a broader electronics manufacturing services (EMS) player — it designs and builds products for other brands rather than selling primarily under its own. Revenue flows from a **Product segment** (finished ACs, washing machines, air coolers — ~80% of Q1 FY27 revenue, ACs alone ~69%), plus plastics injection molding, electronics/PCB assembly, and OEM. Customers are the large consumer-durables brands (Voltas, Blue Star, Godrej, Haier and others). Economics are volume × realization on thin manufacturing margins; the business depends on utilization of a fast-expanding, capital-heavy asset base and on its **ODM mix** (own-design earns more and stickier than build-to-print OEM).

This is an **asset-heavy, working-capital-intensive manufacturer on a powerful secular tailwind with a brutal seasonal overlay.** The AC business concentrates profit in the Mar–Jun summer, so a weak summer wrecks the year — exactly FY26: an early monsoon ended the 2025 season abruptly, leaving ~₹1,300 cr of unsold AC inventory (vs ₹368 cr prior year); FY26 revenue rose ~9% but **net profit fell ~32%** (₹288 cr → ₹197 cr) and OPM fell to ~7%. Structural features colouring everything: **extreme valuation (~88× earnings) on depressed cyclical profits; low ROE (~6.7%) / ROCE (~10%); years of deeply negative free cash flow; recurring equity dilution; and a steep multi-year fall in promoter holding.**

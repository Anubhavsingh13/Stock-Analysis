## Executive Summary
Marine Electricals is a small-cap order-driven electrical company — half proprietary-product maker (marine/defence navigation & power systems), half turnkey electrical EPC (data-centre, semiconductor, industrial power distribution) — riding two genuine secular capex tailwinds (data centres + naval indigenisation) with a strong 2.1× book-to-bill order backlog (₹2,073 Cr). The thesis risk is not the demand — it is that (a) the business is severely **working-capital-intensive** (debtor days ~178, cash conversion has chronically lagged reported profit), (b) margins are thin (~10–11%) and ROE is only ~13%, and (c) the stock is priced at **81× earnings / 10.7× book**, discounting flawless 40%+ multi-year growth. At ₹376 the price has run ~150% in a year and prices in perfection; the business is improving but the margin of safety is negative.

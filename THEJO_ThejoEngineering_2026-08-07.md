## Business Primer
Thejo designs and manufactures rubber- and polyurethane-based engineering products used to keep bulk-material-handling systems running: conveyor belt splicing/repair, belt cleaners and pulley lagging, transfer-point and skirt-sealing solutions, abrasion/wear protection (mill linings, engineered chutes, wear panels), corrosion protection, and dust-suppression fogging systems. Customers are industrial B2B — mining, steel, cement, power and ports — ~600 across India and export markets. Revenue flows through three segments (Manufacturing, Services, Others); the **services/aftermarket** stream (on-site installation, maintenance, re-lagging) is the economic jewel because conveyor wear parts are consumables that must be replaced continuously as long as the customer's plant is running — a replacement annuity tied to the customer's *production throughput*, not to its capex.

This is a **capital-light quality compounder / industrial-consumables** business. The key value driver is **volume + mix** (higher-value engineered products and rising exports, e.g. the first Vale-Brazil order), not price. The key risks are (a) end-market cyclicality — demand ultimately tracks mining/steel/cement production and infra capex; (b) small-cap scale and thin liquidity (FII 0.85%, ~8,000 shareholders); and (c) a rich valuation that leaves the stock exposed to multiple de-rating on any growth or margin wobble. Structurally, promoter integrity and capital allocation have been exemplary, which is what justifies a premium — just not any premium.

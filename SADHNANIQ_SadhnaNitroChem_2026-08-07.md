## Executive Summary
Sadhana Nitro Chem is a small Maharashtra-based fine/specialty-chemical intermediate maker whose business **effectively imploded in FY26**: a fatal fire at its sole Roha plant (Sep-2024, 3 contract-worker deaths), followed by regulatory inspections and local agitation, collapsed revenue **70% to ₹49.67 Cr** (from ₹165.55 Cr) and produced a **net loss of ₹86.42 Cr**. The company has since **recapitalised via a highly dilutive 8:1 rights issue (₹263.5 Cr at ₹1/share)**, ballooning the share count ~9x to ~303 crore, and is pivoting to a **greenfield nitrocellulose plant** that does not yet exist. **The single biggest risk is that the equity is a broken, pre-revenue turnaround speculation trading at ~₹930 Cr market cap (~18x collapsing sales, loss-making, interest coverage <1x) — a valuation with no fundamental support.** Verdict: **AVOID.**

## BUSINESS PRIMER

Sadhana Nitro Chem (incorporated 1973, CIN L24110MH1973PLC016698) manufactures **fine and specialty chemical intermediates** — historically nitrobenzene-chain derivatives such as meta-amino phenol (MAP, ~500 TPA), ODB-2 (a dye/leuco-dye intermediate), metanilic acid, amino phenols, and related nitro/amine compounds — from a **single manufacturing complex at Dhatav MIDC, Roha (Raigad, Maharashtra)**. Revenue is B2B and volume × realisation driven, sold to pharmaceutical, agrochemical, dyes/pigments and performance-chemical customers. Historically ~60% of sales were domestic and ~40% exports (Netherlands, Thailand, US, Belgium, Switzerland, China, Japan). The economics depend almost entirely on **a handful of niche intermediate molecules produced at one site** — a structural single-asset, single-cluster concentration that became the central risk when that site suffered a fatal accident.

This is a **sub-scale, cyclical, commodity-leaning fine-chemicals business** — not a branded compounder and not a wide-moat specialty player. Its key value drivers are product mix (ODB-2/MAP realisations) and plant utilisation; its key risks are **operational/EHS (environment-health-safety) hazard, customer concentration, and cyclicality.** The entire analysis must be read through one dominating structural fact: after the September 2024 fire, **operations were largely halted through FY26, net worth was eroded by losses and then rebuilt by a rescue rights issue, and the investment case has mutated from "operating chemical company" into "distressed recapitalisation + unbuilt greenfield nitrocellulose bet."** The reported market capitalisation (~₹930 Cr) is wildly disconnected from current fundamentals, indicating the price is set by penny-stock speculation, not earnings.

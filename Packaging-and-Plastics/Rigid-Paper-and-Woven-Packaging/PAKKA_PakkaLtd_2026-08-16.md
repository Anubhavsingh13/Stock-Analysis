## Business Primer
Pakka makes eco-friendly packaging from agricultural residue: moulded-fibre (bagasse) plates/bowls/tableware, compostable flexible packaging and films (its differentiated growth engine), and a legacy paper & pulp operation that remains the profit anchor. It also runs a "Food Services" arm. Revenue is B2B and increasingly B2B2C, sold to QSR/foodservice, brands seeking plastic alternatives, and export markets, plus domestic paper. The economics are volume × realisation × mix, with the compostable-flexibles line as the intended high-value future and paper/pulp as the current cash generator. The business depends on (a) converting the plastic-substitution tailwind into profitable volume, and (b) executing a very large capacity expansion (Project Jagriti — new paper machine, power/recovery boilers, and a compostable flexible-packaging facility in Ayodhya) without a balance-sheet accident.

This is an **asset-heavy, capex-cycle, small-cap turnaround with a strong secular theme but weak current returns and a stressed promoter balance sheet.** The value driver is the mix-shift to compostable flexibles; the risks are execution/ramp risk on a debt-funded greenfield, a 16.95% cost of refinanced debt crushing interest cover, and — the dominant risk — **promoter financial stress signalled by a 76.9% pledge and a falling promoter stake.** The structural feature that colours the entire analysis is that reported earnings have already broken (moulded-products segment is loss-making; only paper/pulp is profitable), so the company is spending heavily into a downturn in its own P&L — a fragile combination.

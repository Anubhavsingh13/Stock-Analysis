## Executive Summary
Aarti Surfactants is a **sub-scale commodity surfactant maker** (ionic/non-ionic surfactants for home & personal care, industrial, agro, oil), spun out of Aarti Industries in a 2018 demerger. It has grown revenue impressively — **FY26 ₹859 Cr, +30% YoY** — but **cannot convert it to profit: operating margin is just ~5%, net profit only ₹12.7 Cr, ROE ~5%, ROCE ~8.6%** (below cost of capital). It has **no specialty franchise, no pricing power, and essentially zero institutional interest** (FII 0.08%, DII 0.00%), with a comparatively low promoter stake (~49.8%) and a ~50% retail float. The balance sheet is adequate (D/E ~0.42x) and working capital improved, but the core problem is structural: this is a **price-taking commodity toller** earning single-digit returns. It trades at ~1.4x book — optically "cheap on assets," but the assets earn ~5% — the classic value trap. Only a durable, industry-wide surfactant-margin inflection *plus* operating leverage on its revenue growth would change the picture, and there is no evidence of that yet. **Avoid** as a 2–3 year investment.

## Module 6 — Earnings Quality, ROIC & DuPont
- **Returns: structurally weak.** ROE ~5.3% (FY26), 3-yr ~7%; ROCE 8.6% — **at or below WACC → value-neutral-to-destroying.**
- **DuPont (FY26):** ROE ≈ Net margin 1.5% × Asset turnover 1.66x × Equity multiplier 2.11x ≈ ~5%. The wafer-thin net margin is the binding constraint; leverage is doing part of the (already low) work.
- Profit history is **volatile and going nowhere**: FY21 ₹22 Cr → FY22 ₹6 → FY23 ₹13 → FY24 ₹22 → FY25 ₹15 → FY26 ₹13 Cr. Revenue tripled since FY19; profit essentially flat.
- **Earnings quality: honest but poor** — no operating leverage is reaching the bottom line.

## Module 11 — Forward View & Decision
**What would have to change:** a durable, industry-wide surfactant-margin up-cycle **plus** Aarti capturing operating leverage on its 30% revenue growth to lift OPM toward double digits and ROE toward mid-teens. There is **no evidence** of this yet; the six-year record is flat profit on tripled revenue.
**Catalysts (low probability):** surfactant-margin cycle turn; specialty/value-added mix development; capacity/utilisation-led operating leverage; acquisition interest (low float).
**Risks:** margins stay at ~5%; commodity price-taker economics persist; micro-cap illiquidity; no institutional sponsorship to re-rate the stock.

**Decision: AVOID.** Conviction **3/10**. A clean-books but **no-moat, sub-WACC commodity micro-cap** trading near book on trough earnings. Cheap-on-assets is not the same as cheap-on-value when the assets earn 5%. Only a proven, sustained margin inflection would move this from "value trap" to "deep-cyclical turnaround" — revisit only if OPM holds double digits and ROE crosses ~12% for a few quarters.
**Suggested size:** 0% (avoid; speculative deep-value watchlist at most).

## Executive Summary
Platinum Industries is India's **third-largest PVC/CPVC additives (heat-stabiliser) maker** (~13% share), near debt-free, IPO'd March 2024, now doubling capacity (Palghar 120,000 MTPA live May-2026; Egypt 60,000 MTPA commercialising Q3 FY27). The bull case is a genuine, capacity-led volume story riding PVC/CPVC pipe demand, non-lead regulatory transition and CPVC import-substitution. The problem is what the numbers already show: **EBITDA margin has structurally reset from ~23% (FY23–24) to ~13–15%** (now management's *own* guided range), **profit has plateaued (₹44→50→51 Cr across FY24–26 despite revenue rising ₹264→450 Cr)**, working capital has blown out (WC days −1 → **149**; debtor days 74 → 101), **operating cash flow was negative in FY25 and FY26**, and **FIIs have exited (5.09% → 0.35%)**. At ~25x trailing P/E and ~3x book on flat earnings, the price already embeds a successful ramp. Real business, real catalyst — but wait for margins, cash flow and delivery-vs-guidance to inflect before paying up.
